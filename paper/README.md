# The manuscript moved

The PRC manuscript, its figures and the referee reviews now live in their own
repository:

    ../HGNDPaper

It was split out with `git subtree split`, so its 17 commits of history came
with it. Nothing was re-imported as a single squashed commit.

## Why

The text is revised and circulated on a different cadence from the analysis,
and co-authors who only touch the manuscript should not have to clone the graph
caches, the checkpoints or the reduction products.

## If you change a number here

Figures in `../HGNDPaper/figs/` are generated by this repository. Regenerate
them here, copy them across, and say in the manuscript commit which analysis
commit produced them:

| manuscript element | produced by |
|---|---|
| impact-parameter control, truth-level scan | `scripts/plot_b_full.py` → `results/b_full_analysis/` |
| reconstruction ladder, energy response | `scripts/plot_reco_experiment.py` → `results/reco_experiment/` |
| retrained estimator, held-out sensitivity | `scripts/plot_retrain_eval.py` → `results/retrain_eval/` |

`docs/HGND_PROGRESS_GUIDE.md` is the decision record for what is established,
what has been withdrawn, and what blocks a measurement. It governs both repos.
