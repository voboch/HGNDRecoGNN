## Content census, specified before measurement

Consumer: the DCM independence claims and the statistical unit of the SMASH
truth scan. Inputs are the three delivered DCM directories and the 568
primary files named by the existing SMASH inventory. Inputs are read-only.
The old campaigns and their frozen source trees are not changed.

Prediction: the student's noise-free DCM export contains repeated hit
multisets, including copies assigned to different partitions. The reviewer
predicts about 79,324 distinct hit/truth event signatures out of 197,027 rows;
the exact value depends on which fields define identity and must be measured.
The reported between-directory proxies do not establish exact full-event
identity. No prediction of zero SMASH duplicates is assumed.

Primary identity is the sorted multiset of every exported physical hit field
before time noise (DCM) or every primary-particle field (SMASH), excluding only
event/record identifiers. SHA256 hashes use finite little-endian float64 data,
canonical row order, a field-name header and an explicit record count. Signed
zero is normalised. No rounding or tolerance is used. A second SMASH identity
excludes the vertex coordinates to detect a reused collision with a translated
vertex. DCM hit identity alone cannot establish primary-particle lineage.
The student census additionally retains a signature including truth attributes.
Reported counts concern represented exported events: a zero-particle collision
has no row and cannot be inferred from the primary CSV.

Acceptance: all declared files must be readable and hashed; all rows finite;
file and event counts accounted for; smoke must exercise parsing, both identity
definitions, aggregation and export. Report within-file, across-file and
across-directory overlap separately. No significance test and no AUC acceptance
threshold applies to this deterministic census. It does not train a model or
select an energy window. Empty files and missing fields fail explicitly.

Budget: one CPU smoke on test (three DCM hit files, one primary file per
potential, <=15 min), then one full CPU job (<=60 min), at most one census job
at a time, zero GPUs. The full job requires a successful smoke receipt and an
independent preflight review. Use node-local outputs and one verified export.
Any parse, count, checksum or I/O failure stops the stage; no automatic retry.
The immutable archive hash and exact Slurm receipts will be recorded here.
